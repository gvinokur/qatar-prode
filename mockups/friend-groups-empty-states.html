How It Works

Choose your path and get started in minutes

🛠️ Create a Group
🔗 Join a Private Group
🌍 Join a Public Group
1
✏️

Create Your Group

Click "Create Group", give it a name, and choose whether it's private or public.

Tip: Private groups require an invite link. Public groups appear in the directory for anyone to join.
2
📤

Share the Invite Link

Share your unique invite link via text, email, or social media. Friends click it to request to join.

For private groups: Only people with the link can request access. You can revoke the link anytime.
3
✅

Approve Members

You'll receive notifications when people request to join. Review and approve members to add them to the group.

Important: As admin, you're responsible for managing the approval queue. Members can't join until you approve them.
4
🏆

Start Competing

Once members are approved, everyone makes predictions and the leaderboard updates automatically. Let the games begin!

Ongoing: Continue managing members, customize settings, and set group prizes.
1
💬

Get an Invite Link

Ask the group admin to send you the invite link. They can find it in their group settings.

Note: Private groups don't appear in public searches. You must have the invite link to request access.
2
🔗

Request to Join

Click the invite link, review the group details, and submit your request to join.

Preview first: You'll see the group name, member count, and description before requesting.
3
⏳

Wait for Approval

The group admin will review your request. You'll get notified when they approve or deny it.

Patience: Approval times vary by admin. Check back or wait for the notification!
4
🎉

You're In!

Once approved, you're part of the group. Make predictions and see how you stack up on the leaderboard!

Automatic: Your existing predictions count toward the group leaderboard immediately.
1
🔍

Browse Public Groups

Click "Discover Public Groups" to see all open groups. Filter by size, activity, or tournament.

Easy discovery: Public groups appear in the directory—no invite link needed! See member counts and descriptions.
2
👀

Request to Join

Click any group to see details and description. When you find one you like, submit your request to join.

Join multiple: You can request to join as many groups as you want—private or public!
3
⏳

Wait for Approval

The group admin will review your request. You'll get notified when they approve you.

Admin-managed: Even public groups require approval to maintain group quality and prevent spam.

🎉

Start Competing!

💡 Design Notes & Considerations

  • Sidebar Card Strategy: Keep it concise but compelling. The "Learn more" link bridges to the landing page where users can get full details.
  • Value-First Messaging: Lead with benefits ("Compete with Friends") not features ("Create a Group"). Focus on outcomes users care about.
  • Visual Hierarchy: Icons help scan quickly. Checkmarks in sidebar show quick wins without overwhelming.
  • Multiple Entry Points: Different users have different goals (create private vs. discover public). Offer both paths equally.
  • How It Works Section (NEW): Tabbed interface addresses the mechanics gap. Users can self-select their scenario (create/join private/join public) and get step-by-step guidance. Each step includes practical tips in blue notes.
  • Request/Approval Flow (CRITICAL): All groups (private AND public) require admin approval—no auto-join. This is clearly communicated with dedicated "Wait for Approval" steps and notes about admin responsibility.
  • Admin Responsibility Emphasized: "Create a Group" flow explicitly calls out that admins must manage the approval queue. Sets expectations that this requires ongoing attention.
  • Private vs Public Distinction: Framed as discoverability difference only. Private = need invite link to request; Public = appears in directory to request. Both require approval.
  • Progressive Disclosure: Hero → Why (features) → How (mechanics) → Inspiration (use cases) → Conversion (CTA). Natural flow from emotional to practical to action.
  • Use Cases Section: Helps users visualize themselves using the feature. "Oh, that's perfect for my office pool!"
  • Social Proof Opportunity: Could add "1,234 active groups" or "Join 5,678 players in groups" to show activity.
  • Alternative for Sidebar: Could show a mini-preview of what a populated leaderboard looks like (with blurred/sample data) instead of just icon.
  • Personalization Idea: If user has predictions but no groups, could say "You've made X predictions - see how you compare to friends!"
  • Interactive Tabs: Reduces cognitive load by showing only relevant steps. Users don't have to read all scenarios—just the one they care about.
