## Quick Summary
Adds an "incomplete user" Action Center layout for users who haven't yet completed their pre-tournament predictions (< 30% games OR < 90% QT/awards). During the pre-tournament phase, these users see: a Tutorial CTA card, 3 large instructional track cards (Matches, Qualified Teams, Awards) with scoring rules + progress bars + CTAs, a "Learn more" link in the empty Friend Groups state, and the Recent Results section hidden. The "Opening Game" card and normal carousel are hidden for this user segment. Feature supports both English and Spanish.
